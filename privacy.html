Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

NTFX Google Drive Archive is a personal application used by the account owner to access and manage files within their own Google Drive account using rclone.

Google Drive Access

The application may access Google Drive files in order to upload, download, verify and manage files requested by the account owner.

Use of Google User Data

Google user data accessed through this application is used solely for the personal file-management and archival functions requested by the account owner.

Data Sharing

Google user data is not sold, rented or shared with unrelated third parties.

Authentication

Google OAuth credentials and authentication tokens used by rclone are stored locally on the owner's computer and are used only to authenticate access to the owner's Google Drive.

Data Retention

Files uploaded to Google Drive remain under the control of the Google account owner and may be deleted by the owner at any time.
